Studying Medicine

What is medical school actually like?

An introduction to studying medicine at university, from teaching and placements to exams and student life.

What Medical School Is Like

Medical school combines scientific learning, practical teaching and clinical experience. The balance changes throughout the course.

A Typical Week

Depending on the university and year, students may have lectures, small-group teaching, practical sessions, clinical placements and independent study.

What You Study

  • Anatomy
  • Physiology
  • Biochemistry
  • Genetics
  • Clinical medicine
  • Ethics and communication
  • Public health

Placements

Clinical placements allow students to learn from healthcare professionals and observe how healthcare is delivered in real settings.

Exams and Assessments

Medical students can encounter written exams, practical assessments and clinical assessments such as OSCEs.

Student Life

University life also includes societies, sports, friendships, accommodation and activities outside medicine.

Costs and finance

Studying medicine involves tuition and living costs, but there are different forms of student finance, scholarships and university support.

Always check current information

Fees, maintenance support, bursaries and eligibility rules can change. RouteMed should direct students to official government and university sources for current figures.

Healthcare Careers

Medicine is one route into healthcare.

Explore a range of healthcare careers and understand how they differ.

Medicine

Doctors diagnose, manage and treat patients across a wide range of specialties.

Nursing

Nurses provide care, support treatment and work across many healthcare environments.

Dentistry

Dentists focus on oral health, prevention and treatment.

Pharmacy

Pharmacists specialise in medicines, their safe use and patient care.

Physiotherapy

Physiotherapists help people improve movement and physical function.

Radiography

Radiographers use imaging and other techniques as part of patient care.

Paramedic Science

Paramedics provide emergency and urgent care in a range of settings.

Biomedical Science

Biomedical scientists work with laboratory science that supports diagnosis and research.

Occupational Therapy

Occupational therapists support people with everyday activities and independence.

Speech and Language Therapy

Speech and language therapists support people with communication and swallowing difficulties.

Healthcare Science

Healthcare scientists work across areas such as laboratory science, physiology and genetics.

Psychology

Psychology explores behaviour, cognition and mental processes and has applications across healthcare and wider society.
